## 1. Governing delivery rule

Assume corporate mail security blocks or quarantines attachments.

The first contact must therefore stand on its own as plain email/form text with only three ordinary public HTTPS links:

1. **The Book of Answers** — `https://flipgazine.pages.dev/answers.html`
2. **Voice** — `https://flipgazine.pages.dev/voice.html`
3. **Public Voice Benchmark** — `https://flipgazine.pages.dev/voice.html#voice-benchmark`

Do **not** attach or first-link:

- ZIP files;
- HTML dossiers;
- Data Room;
- Human Eval Sample Record;
- private corpus samples;
- machine-audit exports;
- pricing sheets;
- term sheets;
- license drafts;
- security-architecture internals.

Remove phrases such as **“I’ve attached…”** completely.

The approved closing pattern is:

> **I can provide methodology, provenance evidence and representative records in whatever format is compatible with your organization.**

If a recipient replies with interest, ask which transfer method and file formats their security policy accepts. The dossier, Human Eval record, technical-pilot evidence and private samples are second-stage diligence assets.
